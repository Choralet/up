# Roadmap: every skill in order (for review)

**Status: draft for your review. Nothing is built yet.**

## Where this comes from

- **The order** of the 50 skills is taken from STRIQfit's three videos (the video chapters):
  [Year 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2JHDavNZB4) · [Year 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWXMkzPBpWU) · [Year 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XTuqnzVzGK4).
- **Prerequisites and steps are not from the videos.** YouTube blocks reading the transcript, so they come from standard progressions (see `docs/SKILLS.md`, `docs/RESEARCH.md`) and common coaching practice. Every number is an editable default, like all goals in Up.
- **(?)** marks a chapter name that is ambiguous. I picked the most likely meaning; the list at the end asks you to confirm.

## How it works in the app (approved design)

- Skills tab gets a switch: **My Skills | Roadmap**. Roadmap lists Year 1 → 2 → 3 in the video's order, with "N of M done" per year.
- Each skill shows a status: **Done**, **Training**, **Ready**, or **Locked · Needs: …**.
- Tap a skill: its prerequisites (✓ or not yet), its steps with goals, a form cue, and a **Train This** button (makes it one of your 2 active skills, so it appears in that day's workout with the timer or rep stepper). A Ready skill also offers **I can already do this** (marks its steps done after a confirmation).
- **Links** reuse what the app already has. "Link: step" means the skill is done when that existing exercise is done; training it uses the existing exercise or skill.
- **New** moves become new skill steps. They appear in Roadmap, Skills and your workouts, but **not** in the Push/Pull/Legs/Core tree drawing (keeps the tree readable). New steps added to an *existing* skill (for example Straddle front lever) do appear in the tree.
- **Day** = which workout the skill is trained in (Push, Pull or Legs + Core).
- Goals: `3 × 20 s` = 3 holds of 20 seconds; `3 × 8` = 3 sets of 8 reps; "/side" = each side counts separately.

## Totals

- 50 roadmap skills: 19 link to what Up already has, 31 are new.
- About 55 new steps. Three of them extend existing skills and appear in the tree: Dragon flag (full), Straddle front lever, One-leg planche. Nothing else in the tree changes.
- Linked skills keep the requirements they already have in the tree (new Roadmap-only moves never lock an existing tree exercise).
