### Task 1: Smarter goals (goal ramp)
- `Progress.goalStage: Record<nodeId, number>` (0, 1, 2 = final). A node reached by **level-up** starts at stage 0; nodes without an entry are at the final stage (so beginner roots, onboarding placements and old saves keep full goals).
- `effectiveGoal(node, stage)`: reps → targets `[max(1, round(t*0.6)), max(1, round(t*0.8)), t]`; holds the same ratios (seconds). Sets unchanged.
- Logging: when today's sets meet the **final** goal → level-up suggestion (unchanged). When they meet the **current stage** but not final → stage + 1 and a quiet "Goal up: 3 × 8 next time" note on the Log screen.
- The applied goal (overrides included) is what screens show and what `goalMet` uses.
- Tests: stage after level-up, stage-up on meeting stage goal, final goal still offers level-up, sanitize of goalStage.
